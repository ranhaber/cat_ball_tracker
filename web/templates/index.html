📖 User Guide

🎬 Video Stream Tab

View live camera feed with detection overlays. Detected objects are highlighted with bounding boxes and tracking IDs.

🗺️ Position Map Tab

Top-down view of your yard showing detected objects in real-world coordinates (meters). Requires calibration to work.

⚙️ Control Panel Tab

The Control Panel has 3 sub-tabs:

  • 🎯 Detection:
    • Switch between Cat 🐱 and Ball 🏀 detection
    • Draw a polygon on the camera view to limit the detection area
  • 📐 Calibration:
    • Set up 4 reference points to convert pixels to meters
    • Required for the Position Map to work
  • ⚡ Settings:
    • Adjust resolution, FPS, and frame skip for performance
    • View system status and statistics

📐 How to Calibrate the Camera

Calibration converts pixel positions to real-world meters. Follow these steps:

  1. Place 4 markers in your yard at known positions (e.g., corners of a rectangle)
  2. Measure their positions from a reference point (0,0) in meters
  3. Go to Control Panel → 📐 Calibration sub-tab
  4. Click 📷 Refresh Snapshot to capture the current camera view
  5. Click on each marker in the snapshot image
  6. Enter the X,Y coordinates in meters when prompted
  7. Click Save Calibration

Example: For a 10m × 8m yard with camera at corner:

Point 1: (0, 0)   - bottom-left corner
Point 2: (10, 0)  - bottom-right corner
Point 3: (10, 8)  - top-right corner
Point 4: (0, 8)   - top-left corner

💡 Tip: Use tape or cones as markers. The more accurate your measurements, the better the position tracking!

🎯 Tips for Best Results

  • Lower resolution = faster detection on Pi Zero 2W
  • Frame skip of 2-3 is a good balance
  • Make sure lighting is consistent for reliable detection
  • Calibration points should be spread across the camera view
